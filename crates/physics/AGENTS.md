# capy_physics

Physics simulation and collision detection.

## Scope

- Rigid body dynamics.
- Collision detection and response.
- Raycasting and spatial queries.
- Physics world stepping and configuration.
- Collider shapes and material properties.

## What Does NOT Belong Here

- Rendering debug shapes (belongs in `capy_render`).
- Game-specific physics interactions or gameplay rules (belongs in `capy_game`).
- Character controllers or movement logic driven by game design (belongs in `capy_game`).
